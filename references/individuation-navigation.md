# Individuation

Use for: Conflicting commitments or a wish for greater integration.

Treat individuation as a Jungian ideal of integration, not a measurable diagnosis, a fixed life-stage sequence, or permission to abandon obligations.

## Approach

1. Name the opposing needs without deciding that one is the true self.
2. Ask which needs have space in daily life and which are habitually excluded.
3. Explore whether coexistence is possible and what practical tradeoffs remain.
4. Let the user choose whether the result should be a question, a conceptual distinction, or a feasible experiment.

Wholeness here means making room for complexity. It does not require perfection, special destiny, reconciliation with an unsafe person, or acting on every impulse.

For historical attribution, consult the [research index](research/README.md) and the relevant source before asserting that Jung made a particular claim.
