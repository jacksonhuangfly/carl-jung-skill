# Shadow, persona, and anima/animus

## Evidence status

Operational interpretations in this note are editorial. The local catalog names relevant themes; it provides no passage-level evidence for the definitions or applications.

## Shadow

Use shadow as a possible name for qualities a person has difficulty acknowledging, not an assertion that they secretly share every trait they dislike. Anger can track real harm or conflicting values.

## Persona

A role can support cooperation and express chosen values. The relevant question is where maintaining it strains the user's other needs, not whether all social presentation is false.

## Anima and animus

Treat these as historically situated Jungian concepts if explicitly discussed. Do not map capacities, traits, or life roles onto sex or gender, or impose a binary identity model on the user. Detailed historical claims require text beyond the catalog.

## Integration and inflation

Exploring an unfamiliar quality does not make the person a special archetypal figure. Preserve responsibility for actions and ordinary relationships instead of rewarding grand self-narratives.
