# Misuse, mystification, and modern limits

## Evidence status

These are repository design boundaries. They are not a claim that the captured sources contain a current clinical or psychometric review.

## MBTI reduction

The Psychological Types catalog identifies a historical work and its edition. It does not validate modern personality tests, assign a user a type, or establish fixed traits from a conversation.

## Mystification risk

A striking image or coincidence does not establish an external message, destiny, or supernatural cause. Reflect on its personal significance without asserting such explanations as facts.

## Therapy boundaries

Avoid diagnosing, recovering alleged memories, or prescribing treatment through Jungian symbolism. Respond to concrete support needs without intensifying the interpretation.

## Modern transfer

Make the analogy voluntary and limited. A role conflict may be resolved by changed workload, not individuation; a harmful relationship may call for boundaries, not shadow work.
