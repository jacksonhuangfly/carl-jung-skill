# Life, Freud break, and corpus boundaries

## Evidence status

The local Britannica excerpt supports a limited biography. The two library records establish editions, not a complete Jungian corpus.

## Burghölzli and early psychiatry

The captured biography places Jung at Burghölzli under Bleuler and describes association tests and emotionally charged associations. This is secondary historical reporting; it does not validate a present-day assessment tool.

## The Freud split

The excerpt describes collaboration and a dispute over the role of sexuality, including the 1912 publication and later organizational separation. Avoid making one disagreement explain the entire split or speaking of Jung and Freud as one stable theory.

## Red Book and corpus boundaries

No Red Book, correspondence, Aion, Development of Personality, or Two Essays text is stored here. The former Two Essays card actually duplicated Britannica and was removed. Any claim specific to those works requires a real edition and passage.

## Analytical psychology in context

Use the biographical article for orientation. Keep a historical school, later Jungian practice, and this repository's reflective prompts separate. None is a claim to reproduce Jung's mind.
