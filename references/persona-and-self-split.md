# Persona and self

Use for: Role fatigue, identity strain, or over-identification with a role.

A social role can be useful and chosen. Avoid assuming a hidden single authentic self or treating all adaptation as deception.

## Approach

1. Identify the role, its audience, its demands, and which demands the user endorses.
2. Locate the specific mismatch: values, feelings, time, energy, or expectations.
3. Distinguish a sustainable role from over-identification and from coercive conditions.
4. If action is wanted, consider a small adjustment within the user's real constraints rather than prescribing a dramatic rupture.

For example, a reliable team lead can remain reliable while declining an optional task. Rest and workload changes may matter more than symbolic interpretation.
